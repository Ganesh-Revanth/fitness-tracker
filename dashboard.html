                    How it works

                    About FT26

                    A quick guide to getting the most from your fitness dashboard.

                    Your daily flow

                    Train, track, understand

                    FT26 keeps your workouts, habits, nutrition, and body trends in one place. Use it daily for quick actions, then use Progress to understand the bigger picture.

                    01

                    Start your day

                    Open Dashboard to see today’s workout, checklist, nutrition totals, hydration, streak, and body metrics.

                    02

                    Complete a workout

                    Choose a plan in Workouts, complete each exercise, and tick every finished exercise. A fully completed plan counts toward your streak.

                    03

                    Track daily habits

                    Your default checklist includes protein at twice your body weight in grams and 7-8 hours of sleep. Edit or add habits from Workouts.

                    04

                    Log your records

                    Use Records for individual lifts, cardio sessions, measurements, and dated photo references. Add duration with cardio to unlock pace insights.

                    05

                    Read your progress

                    Progress compares 7, 30, 90, or 365 days with the previous period. Review activity, weight, volume, cardio, measurements, streaks, and personal bests.

                    06

                    Make it yours

                    Set your primary goal and weekly targets in Settings and Progress. Export your records whenever you need a copy of your data.

                    Data and privacy

                    Your account data is stored with your signed-in Firebase account. Weight, workout, nutrition, and record changes sync when you are online; the offline notice tells you when changes may not save.
